# SatyaCheck Android

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Users
Family guardians checking audio and enrolling familiar voices, including people reading under stress. See ../PRD.md and ../web/PRODUCT.md for shared product truth.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Flutter and Kotlin app, Material 3, existing local backend. Same-device cellular call capture is blocked by Android. Present manual recordings and real-backend bundled examples honestly. Microphone permission is necessary for recording; phone and overlay permissions do not enable cellular call audio. Unverified is neutral grey; insufficient audio is unscored. No scoring or backend changes. Recent checks are session-only.

## Brand Commitments
Shared calm, readable SatyaCheck identity with the web workspace, native Android navigation, light/dark schemes and accessible text scaling. User selected code-first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
48 dp controls, system Back and safe areas, large text, Hindi and English recording prompts, semantics and plain-language recovery.

Brand update (2026-10-08): use the user's original logo and the marketing reference at https://satya-check-website.vercel.app/ — warm ivory, ink-blue, electric blue, coordinated dark mode. Bundle assets locally.
