# Signal observatory

## Direction
A quiet instrument for looking closer at a familiar voice. Deep ink, warm porcelain and pale periwinkle; generous editorial type; three interlocking optical rings representing identity, authenticity and intent. The ivory listening desk is the main point of interaction. Horizontal navigation keeps the workspace open, with dedicated routes for voices and evidence.

## System
Manrope is served locally. Display headings use tight tracking and light weight; body copy remains conversational, with sentence-case controls. Ink `#101a2c`, porcelain `#f4f1e9` and periwinkle accents establish hierarchy. Borders are low-contrast, surfaces restrained and primary controls rounded. Detailed tokens, responsive type and spacing are in `src/styles.css`.

The sculpture is decorative and labelled in adjacent content. The important product concepts remain readable without artwork. Hindi, English and Hinglish are supported, with system-script font fallback. Long names wrap; forms and reports collapse into a single column on phones.

## Motion
One finite artwork entrance. Fine-pointer tilt uses damped springs and returns to rest on pointer leave. Shared layout indicators connect navigation and signal tabs. Content reveals run once when entering the viewport. Reduced motion immediately exposes reveal content and disables spatial artwork movement. Processing motion only appears during an actual operation. Verdict numbers do not count up: a score must be readable immediately.

## Product truth
Unknown identity remains neutral. Authority-check results never claim verified identity. Insufficient quality suppresses the score. Synthetic speech is evidence considered alongside intent, not a binary accusation. Sample reports are visibly labelled on-screen and in exported data. Network failures remain visible and recoverable.

## Review
Desktop and phone captures are in `design/review`. Browser checks cover 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixel layouts, long Hindi and Latin names, consent, focus restoration and server error recovery. Generated artwork is documented in `design/ASSETS.md`.
