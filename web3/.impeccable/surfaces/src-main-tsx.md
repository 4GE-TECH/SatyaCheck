# SatyaCheck workspace (web3) and Android companion

Operate surfaces (report, live, voices) with a persuasive home. Guardians check recordings; judges read it from three metres away on a projector. The Flutter app on satyacheck_mobile shares the same world.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The listening room, in "Ethereal Glass" form. Evidence is drawn from the recording itself: a real spectrogram, time-aligned lanes, fusion wires weighted by the actual weights. It refuses the category defaults of a score dial, a hero metric and equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: Near-OLED night ground #050611 with an original WebGL field of voice ribbons that follows the pointer and the microphone, plus fixed grain. Logo voice colours belong to audio and tone colours to verdicts. Geist and Geist Mono. A floating glass island nav with proximity springs, pill buttons with a trailing icon in its own circle, and a double bezel on the composer and the verdict only.
STORY: The home runs in AIDA order: hero with an inline live-wave headline and composer, then the three-signal accordion, a scrubbed principle statement, the advisory marquee, stacking labelled samples, and a closing call to action. The report goes verdict, numbered plan, fusion, scrubber, transcript plus measurements, flat evidence rows.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the headline on the left over 2–3 lines with the inline wave pill, the bezelled composer on the right, the island nav floating above, ribbons behind. Report: the bezelled verdict with an animated trust count on a reference scale.
FORM: Author-original effects in the template categories (ambient field, top dock, inline wordmark wave, stacking cards, scramble-free SplitText reveals). Template sources were unavailable, so nothing claims to be them. gpt-taste roll: editorial split hero, Geist (Cabinet Grotesk was unavailable offline), accordion, marquee, inline type pill, card stacking, scrub reveal. Seed a42fa08d lineage.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
